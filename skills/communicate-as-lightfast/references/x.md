# Company posts and threads

Use for Lightfast X communication. Choose the speaker and one supported point before compressing it. A company announcement, technical explanation and founder reflection require different claims and registers.

- Lead with the actual change, useful observation or question. Explain its consequence through a mechanism or example when space permits.
- Preserve conditions that prevent a metric or availability claim becoming misleading. Link an accepted public source for depth; a link does not excuse an overclaim in the post.
- Use a thread only for a real progression. Each post should advance it; do not fragment one idea to manufacture length or force a promotional ending.
- For replies, inspect available parent/surrounding context and our prior replies. Add information rather than repeating ourselves. State missing coverage outside the draft.
- Verify the applicable limit when needed; do not assume premium posting access. Distinguish a local character count from platform URL/Unicode accounting where relevant.
- Drafting/review does not send, like, follow or schedule. Keep company posts distinct from the optional personal-voice skill.

For concrete patterns, select only the matching example in [Company X examples](company-x-examples.md). A long X Article is not a short post or thread. Media not inspected cannot be used as evidence for a draft claim.
