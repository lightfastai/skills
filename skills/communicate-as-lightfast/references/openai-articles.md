# OpenAI article examples

Five user-selected craft references, reviewed 2 October 2026. These capsules paraphrase public articles; they are not Lightfast facts, a prescribed corporate voice, or language to copy. Borrow the explanatory sequence and verify every Lightfast claim from its own evidence. Source facts are dated examples, not current product guidance.

Coverage: the source research inspected all five article texts. The Sol page also received a visual browser review. Other media observations are mainly text/captions; no claim is made that all video or audio was consumed.

## Introducing dots — human purpose and reviewable work

[Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)

The opening connects persistent assistance to attention and personal goals before explaining the technology. Autonomy, relevance, communication, safeguards, organizational use and availability form a progression. Role-specific scenarios connect input, work performed, reviewable output and human decision. In the scientist example, new data leads into analysis and interpretation while review remains visible. Trust and permissions receive substantive treatment rather than a closing disclaimer. Scenarios show possibilities; internal experience and an early-tester anecdote provide different support from measured case studies. Hero imagery and separate scenarios orient the reader.

**Lightfast adaptation:** open with a recognizable research problem, show what changes for the researcher, then explain the method and controls. Do not turn an illustrative scenario into a claim that an autonomous system is already operating.

## Introducing GPT 6 1 Sol — comparisons with conditions

[Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol/)

A capability-and-cost proposition develops through coding, professional work, computer use, science, factuality and safety. Evaluations carry the argument; benchmark names connect to measured activities, with reasoning settings and cost context retained. Model-positioning and benchmark cards make the evidence hierarchy scannable. Contrary evidence remains visible, including a stronger model's scientific advantage. Methodological qualifications accompany deployment discussion, and selected factuality/safety tasks are limited as representations of ordinary use.

**Lightfast adaptation:** develop a meaningful comparison through distinct tests. Publish settings and contrary findings that affect the conclusion. More experiments help only when their validity and comparability are established. Avoid importing a launch's breadth into a narrow study or implying that one benchmark establishes general research quality.

## Better prompt caching for GPT 6 — bottleneck to diagnosis to intervention

[Source](https://openai.com/index/better-prompt-caching-for-gpt-6/)

A recurring workload produces repeated context, giving computation reuse a practical latency/cost purpose. The explanation moves through workload, mechanism, observation, a diagnostic JSON artifact, interventions and customer outcomes. Detail follows developer decisions: cache boundaries, reasoning adjustments, stable tools and prewarming. Documentation carries exhaustive implementation detail. The diagnostic artifact makes an invisible failure inspectable. Customer metrics are reported testimonials rather than standardized experiments.

**Lightfast adaptation:** this is the closest method-post structure: recurring bottleneck, mechanism, observable failure, intervention and measurement. Show the artifact explaining why the intervention was selected, then identify what its measurement establishes. Do not treat a customer's contextual result as a controlled comparison or substitute a list of optimizations for causal explanation.

## Introducing the Agents API — responsibilities across system boundaries

[Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api/)

Requirements learned from agents at scale lead into a developer offering and a division between managed infrastructure and application responsibilities. Testimonials precede a minimal API example, environment choices, hosted sandboxes, harness capabilities, open source and beta access. An observability investigation gives code a concrete purpose. Later sections unpack context management, tools and parallel subagents; code and architecture captions make boundaries tangible. The example demonstrates an interface. Customer outcomes provide context, but neither independently establishes general reliability. Implementation/documentation links permit deeper inspection.

**Lightfast adaptation:** follow one research episode across execution, evaluation, evidence retention and next-step selection. Explain ownership at each boundary and verify the connections before claiming the whole process works. A working component does not prove an operational end-to-end loop.

## Build more natural voice experiences — behavior through a sequence

[Source: Build more natural voice experiences with GPT-Live-1 in the API](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live-1-in-the-api/)

An experiential benefit introduces a trial, architecture, evaluations, customer accounts, voice choices and integration. Interruptions, thinking pauses, ambient speech and self-corrections turn technical qualities into recognizable situations. Architecture explains coordination problems in chained components and separates conversational interaction from deeper reasoning. Evaluation captions identify tasks and backend configurations. Customer accounts provide contextual outcomes. Trial, audio examples, evaluation panels and integration excerpt serve different explanatory jobs; their presence does not mean this reference review tested or listened to them.

**Lightfast adaptation:** demonstrate change over time with an annotated sequence. A trace of hypotheses, evidence and revised decisions can explain continual research better than a static diagram. Label observed transitions separately from proposed or narrated behavior.
